Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

Privacy Promise: OurMoney is built for household budgeting and expense co-management. OurMoney operates strictly on user-entered data and does NOT connect to bank accounts, read SMS financial messages, or access bank account credentials.

1. Introduction

Welcome to OurMoney ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and financial information you manage within the OurMoney mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is stored and processed, who it is shared with, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

a. Account & Authentication Information

We use Firebase Authentication (a Google Cloud service) to manage sign-in and secure your account. We collect your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID). Passwords are encrypted and handled directly by Firebase Authentication; they are never stored or visible to OurMoney.

b. Household Workspace Information

When you create or join a household workspace, we store household document IDs, member user IDs, and 6-character invite codes used to link your workspace with your household partner.

c. User-Entered Financial Information

To provide budgeting, debt tracking, and spending analytics, we store user-entered data:

  • Expenses: Transaction amounts, spending categories, descriptions, payment methods, transaction dates, payment status (paid or credit), and creator user ID.
  • Budgets: Monthly target spending limits per category.
  • Loans & Liabilities: Lender names, loan types, original principal amounts, outstanding balances, interest rates, repayment methods, and payment history logs.
  • Pending Purchases: Title, category, merchant or person name, purchase amount, due date, and settlement status (PENDING or SETTLED).

Note: We do NOT collect or store bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, PINs, UPI PINs, OTPs, Aadhaar numbers, or PAN numbers.

3. How Information is Used

Your information is processed strictly to provide application functionality:

  • Authenticating your identity and preventing unauthorized account access.
  • Synchronizing financial records in real time between partners in a shared household workspace.
  • Generating category spending charts, budget progress bars, and historical spending analytics.
  • Calculating deterministic loan payoff timelines and debt-free target schedules.

4. Data Storage & Infrastructure

All application data is securely hosted and stored using Google Cloud Firestore infrastructure. Firestore provides enterprise-grade data encryption both at rest and in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

5. Service Providers & Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data. We do NOT share your information with third-party ad networks, marketing partners, or data brokers.

We utilize Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) as our backend infrastructure service provider to store and sync your data securely. Data is processed in accordance with Google Cloud's security and privacy standards.

6. Data Security Practices

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the mobile app and cloud servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Server-Enforced Access Control: Cloud Firestore Security Rules ensure that users can only access data belonging to their verified household workspace.
  • No Sensitive Financial Credentials: Database policies strictly prohibit storing bank credentials, card numbers, or PINs.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You have full ownership and rights to permanently delete your account and personal data at any time.

To delete your account in the mobile app, go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account.

You can also request manual account deletion on our Account Deletion Page or by emailing support.

8. Children's Privacy

OurMoney is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
