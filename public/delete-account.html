Delete Your OurMoney Account

Instructions and options for permanently deleting your account and user data

Method 1: Immediate In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

If you have access to the OurMoney mobile application on your device:

  1. Open the OurMoney app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Under the Privacy & Data section, tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the account deletion warning screen detailing the consequences.
  5. Tap Permanently Delete My Account and confirm in the dialog.

Your login credentials, authentication profile, and user document will be permanently deleted immediately from Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore servers.

Method 2: External Web / Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the mobile application or device, you can submit a manual account deletion request directly to our developer team:

Send an email to aarthyraja9976@gmail.com or support@ourmoney.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Please include the following information in your request:

  • The Email Address registered with your OurMoney account.
  • Your Display Name (optional, to help verify identity).

Our developer support team will verify your request and permanently erase your account credentials and user profile within 7 business days.

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

  • Authentication Credentials: Your email address, password hash, user ID, and Auth record are permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication.
  • User Profile: Your user document (`users/{userId}`) in Cloud Firestore is erased.
  • Solo Household Workspace: If you are in a solo workspace with no other members, your household document and associated expenses, budgets, loans, and pending items are permanently deleted.
  • Shared Household Workspace: Your membership is severed (`memberIds` array updated). If you created the shared household, workspace ownership is transferred to your remaining partner so their access to shared household accounting history is preserved.
