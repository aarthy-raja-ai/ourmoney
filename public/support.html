ℹ️ About OurMoney

OurMoney is a modern, privacy-focused household financial management app designed for couples and individuals. It empowers households to track shared expenses, manage category budgets, monitor active loans and EMIs, and view real-time spending insights without exposing bank credentials or sensitive account numbers.

🔐 1. Account & Login Help

  • Signing Up: You can create an account using your email address and a secure password.
  • Login Issues: If you are unable to log in, ensure your credentials are typed correctly. Use the "Forgot Password" link on the sign-in screen to receive a password reset link.
  • Profile Updates: You can update your display name anytime in the app under Settings → Profile.

🏡 2. Household & Partner Connection

  • Household Workspace: OurMoney allows you to operate in "Solo Mode" or link with a partner in a shared workspace.
  • Connecting a Partner: The workspace owner generates a 6-character invite code under Settings → Household Workspace. The joining partner enters this code under Join Household.
  • Real-Time Synchronization: Once connected, both partners can instantly log and view household expenses, budgets, and loans.
  • Leaving a Household: You can leave a workspace at any time under Settings → Household Workspace → Leave Household Workspace.

💳 3. Expenses & Transactions

  • Logging Expenses: Tap the + button from the Home or Transactions tab to add an expense with amount, category, payment method, date, and notes.
  • Payment Methods: Choose from Cash, UPI, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other.
  • Payment Status: Track entries as Paid or Credit/Pending. You can mark credit entries as settled whenever settled.
  • Editing & Deleting: Tap any expense in the transaction list to view details, update, or delete it.

📊 4. Budgets

  • Setting Category Budgets: Navigate to Insights → Budget Progress → Manage to set target monthly spending limits for categories like Food, Groceries, Transport, and Bills.
  • Budget Utilization Indicators:
    • < 70%: Normal spending pace
    • 70% - 90%: Heads-up notice
    • 90% - 100%: Target almost reached
    • > 100%: Budget target exceeded

🏦 5. Loans & EMIs

  • Tracking Loans: Log active loans (e.g. Home Loan, Gold Loan, EMI, Personal Loan) under the Loans tab.
  • Privacy Protection: OurMoney tracks balances and terms for planning only. Never enter bank account numbers, passwords, or sensitive account credentials.
  • Recording Payments: Select any loan card and tap Record Payment to log EMIs or principal prepayments.

🗑️ 6. Account & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data and can permanently delete your account and household records at any time.

  • In-App Deletion: Open the OurMoney mobile app and navigate to Settings → Privacy & Data Security → Delete Account.
